Newcomer

Moving to a new city? Find the neighborhood that already likes what you like.

Tell Newcomer what you love. It asks Qloo's taste graph where people who share those tastes concentrate, names the neighborhoods, shows the places that prove it, and plans a weekend to test the move before you sign a lease.

Artists, shows, films, foods, activities, up to 8. No personal details, please: the names are looked up in Qloo.

Working on it

    Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors. Teal circles are neighborhoods, sized by taste match; orange dots are the places listed.

    Newcomer uses Qloo for taste data, Open-Meteo for geocoding and OpenStreetMap (Photon) for neighborhood names. Qloo results describe what groups of people tend to like, not what one person will do. For AI agents: Newcomer's own MCP server is at (tool find_neighborhoods).
